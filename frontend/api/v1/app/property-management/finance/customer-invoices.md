# Customer Invoices API

Domain: `Property Management > Finance`

Base route:

`/api/v1/app/{company}/property-management/finance/customer-invoices`

A customer invoice is what the company bills a landlord for its own fees: the **management fee**
(raised with each remittance) and the **letting / re-letting fee** (raised when a new lease starts).
The company is always the seller, the landlord's [customer](../../users/landlords.md#customer-and-quickbooks)
is always the buyer. Each one covers one or more bills whose supplier is the company itself (the
`default_vendor` setting): one bill per line.

Its life:

1. **Generated** from the bill, `unverified`. The management fee and letting fee bills raise theirs
   automatically; any other bill to the company can have one generated by hand.
2. **Verified** by the staff responsible for it (see [Who verifies](#who-verifies)).
3. **Sent to QuickBooks**, which gives it its document number. That number becomes the bills'
   `invoice_number`.
4. **Signed** on the company's ETR device. The CU number becomes the bills' `tax_invoice_number` and is
   written back onto the QuickBooks invoice.

Until it is sent, invoices of the same customer can be **merged**. It can be **cancelled** until it is
signed, and **deleted** once cancelled.

## Endpoints

- `GET /customer-invoices`
- `GET /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}`
- `POST /customer-invoices/merge`
- `PATCH /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/verify`
- `POST /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/send-to-quickbooks`
- `POST /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/sign`
- `PATCH /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/cancel`
- `DELETE /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}`
- `GET /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/export?format=pdf`
- `POST /bills/{bill}/customer-invoice` (generate, see [Bills](bill.md#customer-invoice))

## Permissions

| Permission | Allows |
|---|---|
| `view-customer-invoice` | List, view, print and export |
| `generate-customer-invoice` | Generate one from a bill |
| `merge-customer-invoice` | Merge |
| `verify-customer-invoice` | Verify, when also [responsible](#who-verifies) for it |
| `send-customer-invoice-to-quickbooks` | Send to QuickBooks |
| `sign-customer-invoice` | Sign on the ETR device |
| `cancel-customer-invoice` | Cancel |
| `delete-customer-invoice` | Delete |

Show actions from the record's `permissions` block, not from these names: each flag is `true` only when
the user holds the permission **and** the invoice is in a state the action accepts. `permissions.merge`
is per row; enable the bulk **Merge** action when every selected row has it.

## Statuses

`status` is `{ value, color }`:

| Value | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `unverified` | `secondary` | Generated, waiting for the responsible staff |
| `verified` | `info` | Verified, ready to send to QuickBooks |
| `sent` | `primary` | In QuickBooks, with its QuickBooks number; ready to sign |
| `signed` | `success` | Signed on the ETR device; final |
| `cancelled` | `danger` | Cancelled; its bills are left without a customer invoice |

A send in progress keeps `verified` and shows `quickbooks.queued_at`; a failed one keeps `verified` and
shows `quickbooks.error`. A sign in progress or failed keeps `sent` and shows `etr_status` / `etr_error`.

## List

`GET /api/v1/app/{company}/property-management/finance/customer-invoices`

- Filters:
  - `filter[status]` (one or CSV of the values above)
  - `filter[customer_id]`, `filter[user_id]` (the landlord), `filter[facility_id]` (any line on that property)
  - `filter[fee_type]`: `management-fee`, `letting-fee`, `re-letting-fee` (any line of that type)
  - `filter[quickbooks]`: `sent` or `not_sent`
  - `filter[etr_status]`: `signed`, `not signed`, `failed` (as on [invoices](../lease-management/invoices.md))
  - `filter[created_between]`, `filter[invoice_date_between]`: `from,to`
  - `filter[search]`: number (`CI0012` or `12`), QuickBooks number, CU number, landlord name
- Sort: `sort=id,invoice_date,total,created_at` (default newest first)
- Include: `include=items,customer` (the detail always has both)
- Pagination: `per_page`, `page`

Only invoices with at least one line on a property allocated to the user are listed.

## Who verifies

| The invoice's lines | Verified by |
|---|---|
| Any management fee line, or any line that is none of the three fees | The **payables staff** (`default_account_payables_staff` setting). When that setting is empty, anyone with `verify-customer-invoice` |
| Only letting and re-letting fee lines | The holders of the **Letter of Offer preparation role** (`loo_generation_role_id`) on the lines' properties; when nobody holds it there, everyone with a role on those properties |

The verifier must also hold `verify-customer-invoice`. Generating an invoice gives each verifier a
pending task; verifying, merging or cancelling it clears them.

## Verify

`PATCH /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/verify`

No payload. `unverified` → `verified`, recording who and when.

- `403` with the reason: not unverified (`Only an unverified customer invoice can be verified.`), not a verifier (`You are not responsible for verifying this customer invoice.`), or missing `verify-customer-invoice`.

## Merge

`POST /customer-invoices/merge`

| Field | Required | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `customer_invoice_ids` | Yes | array[int] | At least 2, distinct |

Builds one new `unverified` invoice holding every line of the merged ones (one line per bill, each
keeping its bill's notes). The merged invoices are removed (`merged_into_id` is set on them) and their
bills now point at the new one. The new invoice's notes join theirs, and it needs verifying again.

`403` when any of them is already in QuickBooks, signed or cancelled (`Only customer invoices not yet
sent to QuickBooks can be merged.`), or the user lacks `merge-customer-invoice`.

`422` on `customer_invoice_ids` when any of them:

- belongs to another customer (`Only invoices of the same customer can be merged.`)
- is in another currency (`Only invoices in the same currency can be merged.`)

Response: `{ "message": "Customer invoices merged.", "customer_invoice": { ...detail } }`.

## Send to QuickBooks

`POST /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/send-to-quickbooks`

No payload. Queues the send and answers at once (`quickbooks.queued_at` set, `quickbooks.error`
cleared); poll the detail or refresh. Once QuickBooks accepts it the invoice is `sent`, carries
`quickbooks.doc_number`, and each bill's `invoice_number` becomes that number.

The landlord's customer is made a QuickBooks customer first when it is not one yet. Each line is sent
as the QuickBooks item set for its fee type (settings `quickbooks_item_*`), with the VAT or no-tax code
(`quickbooks_tax_code_*`). A retry never creates a second QuickBooks invoice.

`422` when:

- `403`: it is not `verified` (`Only a verified customer invoice can be sent to QuickBooks.`)
- the company is not connected (`QuickBooks is not connected for this company.`)
- an item or tax code it needs is not set (`Set the QuickBooks item for management fees in the finance settings.` and similar)

## Sign

`POST /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/sign`

No payload. Signs on the company's ETR device (the server's default device, Incotex) with the
company's PIN as seller and the landlord's as buyer, and the QuickBooks number as the invoice number.
Queued like [invoice signing](../lease-management/invoices.md); the response says whether it was signed
already or queued. Once signed the invoice is `signed`, each bill's `tax_invoice_number` becomes the CU
number, and the CU number is written to the QuickBooks invoice's custom field
(`quickbooks_cu_custom_field`, default `Cu Invoice Number`; `quickbooks.cu_synced_at`).

`403` when it is not `sent` (`Only a customer invoice sent to QuickBooks can be signed.`); `422` on `etr`
with the device's reason.

## Cancel

`PATCH /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/cancel`

| Field | Required | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `reason` | No | string | Up to 500 characters |

Allowed while `unverified`, `verified` or `sent`. An invoice already in QuickBooks is **voided** there
first; if QuickBooks refuses, nothing is cancelled (`422` on `quickbooks`). Its bills get back the
invoice number they had before QuickBooks, and are left **without a customer invoice**: one can be
generated from each bill again.

Response message: `Customer invoice cancelled. Its bills are now without a customer invoice; generate one from each bill again.`

`403` `A signed customer invoice cannot be cancelled.` (a signed fiscal invoice needs a credit note).

## Delete

`DELETE /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}`

Only a `cancelled` invoice (`403` `Cancel the customer invoice before deleting it.`).

## Print and export

The bill's **Print invoice** prints its customer invoice when the bill has no uploaded invoice (see
[Bills](bill.md#customer-invoice)). The document type is `customer_invoice`: render it through the
[document templates](../document-templates-frontend.md) endpoint with `resource_id` = the customer
invoice id, or download `GET /customer-invoices/{customerInvoice}/export?format=pdf`. A cancelled
invoice does not print (`422`).

## Where customer invoices come from

- **Management fee**: each remittance that charges a management fee raises a management fee bill to
  the company, posted at once; its customer invoice is generated with it.
- **Letting / re-letting fee**: when a lease becomes active (approved, activated by hand or raised from
  a Letter of Offer), the property's active management contract is read. A new lease is billed the
  letting fee, a renewal the re-letting fee, on one month of the lease's charges (the components
  in the contract's `letting_fee_components`, else all of them; see
  [landlords](../../users/landlords.md#what-a-letting-fee-is-charged-on)): a percentage contract
  charges that percentage of 12 months of them, a fixed contract that many months of them
  (half, one, two months' rent), a custom contract the amount entered; none charges nothing.
  The bill is raised under the property's **Letting Fee Service Contract** (a renewal: **Re-Letting
  Fee Service Contract**) with the company, which is created (at the default tax) when the property
  has none, and carries **that contract's tax**, whatever the company's VAT registration. Its notes
  are `Letting Fee - Lease#{id} {tenant}`; the customer invoice's notes and line read
  `{property} - {bill notes} - {Mon YYYY the lease was made}`. The bill's `lease_id` names the lease.
  It is posted at once and its customer invoice generated with it. A lease has one live letting fee
  bill; once that bill is cancelled the lease can be billed again. From the lease page:
  `POST lease-management/leases/{lease}/letting-fee` (see
  [Leases](../lease-management/leases.md#generate-the-letting-fee)).
- **By hand**: `POST /bills/{bill}/customer-invoice` for any other bill whose supplier is the company.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `php artisan customer-invoices:bill-letting-fees {lease*} [--rebill] [--dry-run]` | Bills the letting (or re-letting) fee of the given leases as their activation would have: the fee bill, posted, and its customer invoice. Same rules as activation: one live fee bill per lease, only when the property's active management contract charges a fee and the company vendor and letting fee expense type are set. `--rebill` cancels a lease's live fee bill and its customer invoice (voided in QuickBooks if sent; a signed one is refused) and bills it again under today's contract and rules, e.g. after "Letting Fee Charged On" was corrected. Each lease is first tried in a rolled-back transaction: `--dry-run` reports that trial, and a real run touches QuickBooks only once the trial has billed |
